Local InstallerSend models directly into your ComfyUI library ▾
The card still has one Download action. AbyssBeacon follows the behavior selected here.
Why use the Local Installer? Downloads handled by AbyssBeacon get live Active Downloads progress, transfer speed, cancel/retry controls, automatic library placement, and optional preview/info sidecars. Browser downloads are handed off to your browser, so AbyssBeacon cannot display byte-level progress for them.
Queued CivitAI/CivitAI Red releases are checked after every completed scan. AbyssBeacon verifies actual download availability, so popularity-based Early Access releases can trigger before their scheduled date.
Choose the main ComfyUI folder only. AbyssBeacon automatically routes model files into models\… and workflows into user\default\workflows.
