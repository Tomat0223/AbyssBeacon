Connect TensorHub Art

Keep this token private. It represents your signed-in TensorHub Art browser session. AbyssBeacon stores it only in your local secrets.json.

First sign in to tensorhub.art. Then copy the complete ta_token_prod cookie value. Do not use a TAMS application/API key.

  1. Open https://tensorhub.art and sign in.
  2. Press F12 and open Storage.
  3. Open Cookies → https://tensorhub.art.
  4. Find ta_token_prod and copy its complete Value.
  5. Paste that value into AbyssBeacon's TensorHub session-token field, Save, then Test.
  1. Open https://tensorhub.art and sign in.
  2. Press F12 / Ctrl+Shift+I and open Application.
  3. Storage → Cookies → https://tensorhub.art.
  4. Find ta_token_prod and copy its complete Value.
  5. Paste it into AbyssBeacon, Save, then Test.

Also applies to Edge, Brave, Opera and most Chromium browsers.

  1. Sign in at tensorhub.art.
  2. Enable developer features in Safari Settings → Advanced if needed.
  3. Develop → Show Web Inspector → Storage.
  4. Select tensorhub.art cookies, find ta_token_prod, and copy its complete value.
  5. Paste it into AbyssBeacon, Save, then Test.

Find CivitAI Red session values

Keep these values private. Copy only the cookie values and paste them into your own local AbyssBeacon.

Sign in to civitai.red first, then find __Secure-civ-token and __Secure-civ-device.

  1. Press F12 and open Storage.
  2. Cookies → https://civitai.red.
  3. Copy the complete Value for both cookie names.
  1. Press F12 / Ctrl+Shift+I and open Application.
  2. Storage → Cookies → https://civitai.red.
  3. Copy the complete values for both cookies.

Also applies to Edge, Brave, Opera and most Chromium browsers.

  1. Enable developer features in Safari Settings → Advanced if needed.
  2. Develop → Show Web Inspector → Storage.
  3. Select civitai.red cookies and copy both complete values.
